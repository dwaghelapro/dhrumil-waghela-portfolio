How I Work

The pattern behind every case above: understand the real workflow first, document it precisely, then stay with the delivery through validation.

01

Discover & Map

Stakeholder workshops, user research and Voice of Customer, then current-state / future-state mapping (BPMN) to find where the workflow actually breaks.

  • Customer journey mapping
  • Root cause & gap analysis
  • Hypothesis-driven priorities
02

Specify & Prioritize

Business needs become BRDs, PRDs and FRDs: user stories into use cases, with acceptance criteria, data mappings and API requirements. Backlog owned daily.

  • User stories & acceptance criteria
  • Process flows in Visio / Lucidchart
  • Roadmap & OKR alignment
03

Deliver & Validate

Sprint planning, UAT strategy and defect triage with engineering and QA, then KPI dashboards in Power BI and SQL to prove the outcome, not just the launch.

  • UAT planning & test cases
  • Release validation
  • KPI, OKR & ROI measurement
